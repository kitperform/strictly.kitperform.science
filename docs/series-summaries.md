# Series summaries

One section per series (1 to 24) summarising what the English Wikipedia page for that series says about dates, the judging panel, the number of couples and weeks, how and when results were given, the elimination mechanism and the structure of the page itself. The summaries were written from the live wikitext read on 8 November 2026 and cross-checked against the HTML captures in `data/wikipedia/series/0NN/`; where the two disagree the text says so. Series 24 was in progress at the time of reading.

The four era notes at the end were written by the researcher responsible for each block of six series and record the cross-series patterns a modeller needs to know. The companion [anomaly register](wikipedia-anomaly-register.md) holds the individual corner cases. Quoted Wikipedia text is data about the source, not instruction. The file is generated by the research pipeline script `gen_docs.py` from the phase-1 research JSON (`historians.json`).

## Series 1

- **Dates:** 15 May 2004 – 3 July 2004 (infobox first_aired/last_aired; num_episodes = 9 = 8 live shows + a separate final results episode). The ONLY spring/summer series; series 2 also aired in 2004 (23 Oct – 11 Dec), so 2004 has two series.
- **Judges:** Craig Revel Horwood; Arlene Phillips; Len Goodman (head judge); Bruno Tonioli
- **Judge count per week:** 4 every week (no guest judges). Infobox lists the panel as Len, Arlene, Craig, Bruno; the parenthesised scores follow Craig, Arlene, Len, Bruno (sentence under ==Weekly scores==).
- **Couples:** 8
- **Weeks:** 8
- **Results show:** No separate results episode until the final: Ratings table has one row per week ('Week N') plus 'Week 8 results' (both dated 3 July). Elimination dates in the Couples table equal the live-show Saturday. Main article says results were announced in a live show one hour later for series 1–4, but the series 1 Ratings table doesn't list separate results rows for weeks 1–7. Ratings dates are {{dts|15 May}} etc. with no year; year only appears in the 'Series average' row. NOTE discrepancy: Ratings row 'Week 5' is dated 13 June, but Couples table says Claire & John eliminated 'on 12 June 2004'.
- **Dance-off and elimination rules:** No dance-off. Judges' ranking points + public-vote ranking points summed; two lowest = 'bottom two'; lowest of those eliminated (public-vote points take precedence on a tie). 'Bottom two' colour (lightblue) marks the survivor, 'Eliminated' (#f4c7b8) the other. Week 1 had NO elimination; the first elimination (22 May, after week 2) used combined weeks 1+2 scores, shown as a '1+2' column in the scoring chart.

- **Format notes:** Week 1 table has no Result column (no elimination). Week 3 staged at Blackpool Tower Ballroom. Week 5: samba + an unscored group Viennese waltz performed by all 5 couples (single multi-couple row, empty Scores cell, Music colspan=2; dance chart shows it as a rowspan=5 'Group Viennese waltz' cell). Weeks 6–7: two unlearned dances each, scoring-chart cell '31+36=67' with {{sort|67|}}. Week 8 Final (2 couples): favourite ballroom + favourite Latin + showdance, ALL THREE SCORED ('36+31+35=102') – the only series in 1–6 where the showdance is scored on every version of the page.
